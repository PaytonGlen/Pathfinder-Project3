Motors
======

The drive system is the TB6612FNG motor driver shield plus the two DC gear motors.

Motor driver pins on the master:
- D3 = STBY
- D5 = PWMA
- D6 = PWMB
- D7 = AIN1
- D8 = BIN1

Drive tuning values we used:
- `LEFT_DRIVE_SPEED = 173`
- `RIGHT_DRIVE_SPEED = 180`
- `TURN_SPEED = 220`

How turning works:
- tank steering is differential drive
- if the tracks move at different speeds, the tank turns
- if they spin opposite directions, the tank pivots in place

Since we do not have encoders, distance and turns are based on time.
