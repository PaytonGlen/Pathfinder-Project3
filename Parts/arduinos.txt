Arduinos
========

We use two Arduino Uno R3 boards.

Piloting Arduino: (Slave device)
- handles the motor driver and tank movement
- reads the front ultrasonic (Front facing) and scan arm sensors
- reads the MPU-6050
- moves the pan/tilt arm
- reads MPU

Devices will communicate via I2C. (Faster communication)

Scouting Arduino: (Master device)
- reads 6 IR Sensors
- reads 5 US Sensors
- sends obstacle distances back to the master



Both boards are standard Uno R3 boards with ATmega328P chips.
