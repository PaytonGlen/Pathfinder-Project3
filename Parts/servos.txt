Servos
======

The scan arm uses two small servos on the master Arduino.

Servo jobs:
- pan servo moves left and right
- tilt servo moves up and down

Pins on the master:
- D10 = pan servo
- D11 = tilt servo

Angles we calibrated:
- `PAN_CENTER = 50`
- `PAN_LEFT = 110`
- `PAN_RIGHT = 0`
- `TILT_LEVEL = 160`

Why the arm matters:
- the tank can stop with the front sensor alone
- the arm is what lets it check both sides and choose where to turn
